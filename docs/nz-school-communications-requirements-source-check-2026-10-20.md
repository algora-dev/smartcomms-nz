# NZ School Communications Requirements Source Check

Reviewed: 20 October 2026

Purpose: source register for `/guides/nz-school-pa-paging-requirements`. The page synthesises official Ministry requirements with public NZ supplier and project material. Supplier examples demonstrate design approaches only. Inclusion is not an endorsement, partnership claim, market-share claim or independent performance test.

## Ministry of Education

### Designing standards for New Zealand schools
Source: https://www.education.govt.nz/education-professionals/schools-year-0-13/property/designing-standards-new-zealand-schools

Checked claims:
- Designing Schools in Aotearoa New Zealand is mandatory for new builds and is to be followed where practicable for maintenance/refurbishment.
- Current 2026 DSNZ section 3.9.3 says public address systems should accommodate fire, lockdown and other school safety warning signals, school bells, voiced announcements and paging.
- Speaker quality and placement must support clear speech intelligibility in learning areas.
- Strobe-light warning systems are required where needed for hearing-impaired learners, and newly installed safety systems should support future strobe additions.

Boundary used on SmartComms:
- These outcome requirements do not mean a normal paging platform is automatically a compliant fire alarm or voice-alarm system.

### Prepare for an emergency or traumatic incident
Source: https://www.education.govt.nz/education-professionals/schools-year-0-13/health-and-safety/prepare-emergency-or-traumatic-incident

Checked claims:
- School emergency plans include communication planning.
- Ministry planning material asks whether warning systems are in place to alert staff and students for evacuation or lockdown/shelter in place.
- The guide distinguishes the emergency procedure from the technology used to communicate it.

### ICT contractor standards and processes
Source: https://www.education.govt.nz/suppliers-and-providers/infrastructure-and-property/school-projects/information-and-communications-technology-contractor-standards-and-processes

Checked claims:
- Data cable installation services, wireless integration services and structured-cabling product supply in schools must be carried out by approved contractors using approved products.
- Ministry technical standards exist for cabling, switching and wireless networks.
- SmartComms wording limits this rule to projects that actually touch Ministry-governed ICT work. It does not state that every PA installer must be a Ministry-approved ICT contractor.

### ICT network upgrades and maintenance
Source: https://www.education.govt.nz/education-professionals/schools-year-0-13/digital-technology/ict-network-upgrades-and-maintenance

Checked claims:
- The school-led network upgrade process includes defining outcomes, gathering current-network information, confirming a design, testing, QA, as-built documents and warranties.
- The SmartComms article uses these as useful project disciplines for network-audio work, without claiming every paging project is formally an ICT network upgrade.

## NZ supplier and project evidence

### Edwards Sound Systems: Three Kings Primary School
Source: https://www.edwardsnz.co.nz/school-uses-paging-system-for-tighter-lockdown-procedures

Checked claims:
- Published 1 March 2024.
- Describes speakers in classrooms/common areas plus four outdoor zones.
- Describes two paging stations, pre-recorded emergency messages and scheduled bells/music.
- Used as an example of defining coverage, multiple trigger locations and everyday/emergency functions together.

### Edwards Netbell
Source: https://www.edwardsnz.co.nz/netbell-school-bell-and-buzzer-systems

Checked claims:
- Public page describes network bell scheduling.
- Says existing bell systems can be automated and Netbell can interface with a PA system.
- Used only to illustrate the control-only / retain-existing-infrastructure upgrade pattern.

### Pacific AV: Ormiston Junior College
Source: https://www.pacificav.co.nz/ormiston-junior-college-auckland/

Checked claims:
- Published project describes FrontRow audio-over-IP paging and bells using the school LAN.
- Describes zone paging, bell schedules, lockdown functions and multiple endpoint/device types.
- Used as a real NZ example of an integrated networked architecture.

### Pacific AV: FrontRow networked audio devices
Source: https://www.pacificav.co.nz/product/frontrow-networked-audio-devices/

Checked claims:
- CM800 description says it can connect the FrontRow IP paging system to standard 100V amplifiers and speakers.
- Used as an example of a hybrid path where useful existing 100V infrastructure may be retained.

### CIE Group / Pure Tech: Thorndon Primary School
Source: https://cie-group.com/project/thorndon-primary/

Checked claims:
- Published case study describes Axis network audio used for school PA and lockdown support.
- Describes indoor ceiling speakers, outdoor horn speakers, a programmable reception microphone, browser-based management, scheduled audio, zones and individual speaker volume control.
- Used to show why outdoor coverage, zoning and volume management belong in the brief.

### SchoolBells.nz
Source: https://www.schoolbells.nz/

Checked claims:
- Public page says its scheduling interface works with existing bell systems / class-change sounders.
- Describes browser scheduling and remote monitoring.
- Used as an example of modernising scheduling without automatically replacing the whole audio system.

### New Era Technology NZ
Source: https://www.neweratech.com/en-nz/resources/blog/pa-bell-systems

Checked claims:
- Public NZ article describes integrated paging, bells, emergency/lockdown capability and VoIP integration as school use cases.
- Used as supporting market context, not as evidence of a mandatory architecture.

## Editorial controls

- No supplier is labelled the universal best option.
- Historical project examples are identified as supplier-published evidence, not SmartComms installations or independent tests.
- Product lifecycle must still be checked in a current proposal.
- Ministry requirements take precedence over supplier claims.
- Fire/building compliance is explicitly kept separate from generic paging/emergency-message capability.
- No pricing was sourced from supplier pages or invented for this guide.
